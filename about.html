About Dr. John Monhollon, MD

Medical Director of Florida Integrative Medical Center

A Rare and Remarkable Healer

"In a world where rushed visits and symptom-masking treatments have become the norm, discovering Dr. John Monhollon is like finding a hidden treasure... He is not just a doctor—he is a true healer, a compassionate soul, and a visionary in integrative and holistic care."

- Zuzana Shaw Novakova

Florida Integrative Medical Center (FLIMC) is a Sarasota-based integrative medical practice led by Dr. John Pylant Monhollon, MD. We focus on personalized, patient-centered care for individuals seeking a more comprehensive approach to health and wellness.

Dr. Monhollon is a board-certified family medicine physician with more than 25 years of clinical experience. He is dedicated to providing care that integrates traditional medicine with functional and integrative approaches. Throughout his career, Dr. Monhollon has focused on helping patients address complex and chronic health concerns through comprehensive evaluation, advanced diagnostics, and individualized care planning.

His approach emphasizes understanding the whole person—not simply managing symptoms—by considering lifestyle factors, nutrition, environmental influences, medical history, and overall wellness goals.

Advanced Experience

Dr. Monhollon has pursued extensive continuing education in integrative medicine, chronic illness, and tick-borne disease management. He completed advanced physician training through the International Lyme and Associated Diseases Society (ILADS) and has subsequently served as a physician mentor for other clinicians seeking advanced education in Lyme disease and related complex chronic conditions.

This experience has made him a sought-after resource for patients seeking a more comprehensive evaluation of chronic and difficult-to-resolve health concerns, such as immune system challenges, tick-borne illnesses, and overall quality of life concerns.

Education & Philosophy

Dr. Monhollon earned his medical degree from St. George's University School of Medicine in 2000. Following his residency in family medicine, he completed an additional year of pathology training to further his understanding of disease processes and diagnostic evaluation. He is affiliated with Sarasota Memorial Hospital and Orlando Health Bayfront Hospital and is a member of the American College for Advancement in Medicine (ACAM).

Known for his compassionate bedside manner and commitment to individualized care, Dr. Monhollon works closely with each patient to develop practical, personalized strategies designed to support long-term health and wellness.
