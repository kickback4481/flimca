EBOO is an advanced ozone therapy that filters the debris of destroyed germs, heavy metals, fungus, and other pathogens. The blood is oxygenated while introducing medical-grade ozone. Some individuals seek EBOO to support immune function and overall wellness.

Ozone Therapy

UBI Therapy

Ultraviolet Blood Irradiation (UBI) is a gentle, non-pharmaceutical therapy that exposes a small amount of your blood to ultraviolet light before reintroducing it into the body. This process is believed to help support immune function, enhance circulation, and promote detoxification as part of a comprehensive wellness approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is functional medicine?
Functional medicine looks beyond symptoms to understand underlying factors that may be affecting your health. At Florida Integrative Medical Center, this approach is paired with personalized care plans, diagnostics, and ongoing support.

What services does FLIMC offer?
FLIMC offers primary care, IV and infusion therapies, and Wellness Week programs designed to support individualized health and wellness goals.

What are infusion therapies?
Infusion therapies deliver nutrients directly into the bloodstream for efficient absorption. At FLIMC, services include options such as Vitamin C, Myers’ Cocktail, NAD+, and other customized nutrient blends based on individual needs.

What is Wellness Week?
Wellness Week is a 5-day intensive program that includes consultation, lab testing, diagnostics, customized infusion therapy, and personalized care planning.

Is IV therapy safe?
IV therapy is administered by trained medical professionals and guided by individual evaluation. Recommendations are made based on each patient’s needs.

How do I get started?
You can request a consultation through our website or contact our office directly to begin your care journey.
