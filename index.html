IV and injectable therapies deliver nutrients directly into your bloodstream for efficient absorption. They are designed to support your body's natural functions. Individual results may vary.

EBOO

EBOO (Extracorporeal Blood Oxygenation and Ozonation) is one of the most advanced forms of ozone-based blood filtration and oxygenation available in integrative medicine.

Read more

EBO2 Therapy

EBO₂ builds upon these same core principles while incorporating an advanced high-performance blood filtration system. As blood continuously circulates through the extracorporeal circuit, it is filtered, oxygenated, and precisely exposed to medical-grade ozone before being returned to the body. This additional filtration component is designed to help reduce the burden of unwanted particles that may accumulate in the bloodstream while supporting healthy circulation, oxygen delivery, and overall physiological balance.

UBI Therapy

Ultraviolet Blood Irradiation (UBI) is a gentle, non-pharmaceutical therapy that exposes a small amount of your blood to ultraviolet light before reintroducing it into the body. This process is believed to help support immune function, enhance circulation, and promote detoxification as part of a comprehensive wellness approach.

Understanding the Difference:
EBOO vs EBO₂

  • Filtration

    EBO₂ incorporates advanced blood filtration as part of the procedure, while traditional EBOO focuses primarily on oxygenation and ozonation.

  • Blood Processing

    EBO₂ is designed to process a larger volume of blood through a continuous filtration circuit during the session.

  • Technology

    EBO₂ utilizes a larger, more advanced extracorporeal filtration system capable of combining filtration, oxygenation, and ozonation into one continuous process.

  • Personalization

    Both therapies are individualized and performed under physician supervision using customized protocols based on each patient's health history, wellness goals, and overall care plan.
